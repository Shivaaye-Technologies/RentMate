Delete in the app

  1. Open SOULMATE.AI and sign in.
  2. Tap your photo in the top-right corner to open Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

What is deleted

Immediately and permanently: your account, profile, photo, location, likes, passes, Super Likes, matches, messages and blocks. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept without the link to your account so we can act on safety issues. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time.

Can't sign in?

Questions or requests about your data: use Report in the app, or delete your account yourself (see below). A support email address will be listed here before public launch.
