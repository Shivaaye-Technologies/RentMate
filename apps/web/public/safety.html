Dating safety tips

Last updated 23 October 2026

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number (112 in India).

Before you meet

  • Keep chatting in SOULMATE.AI until you feel comfortable. Don't share your home address, workplace or financial details early on.
  • Never send money or gift cards to someone you haven't met, however urgent the story.
  • A quick video call is a good way to check someone is who they say they are.

When you meet

  • Meet in a busy public place, and get there and back on your own.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, who you're meeting and when you'll check in.
  • Stay in control of your drink and your belongings. Leave whenever you want to.

If something feels wrong

Use Report and Block from their profile or chat (the ⋯ button). They won't be told, and they will no longer be able to see you or message you.
