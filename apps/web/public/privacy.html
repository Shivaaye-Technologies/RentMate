Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

SOULMATE.AI is a dating service for adults aged 18 and over. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Profile: first name, age, bio, interests, what you're here for, the age range you want to see, and one photo if you add it.
  • Approximate location (optional): only if you tap Show distance. Your phone rounds it to about 1 km before it is sent. Other people only ever see a rounded distance (for example "5 km away"), never your location.
  • Activity: likes, passes, Super Likes and their messages, matches, chat messages, your "ready to meet" choice, blocks and reports you make, and when you were last active (used to show "Online").

Photos

Before upload, your photo is resized and re-encoded on your device, which removes hidden data such as GPS location. Photos are stored privately and can only be viewed by signed-in members through links that expire after one hour. People you block, or who block you, cannot see your photo.

How we use it

  • To run the service: show your profile, suggest people, create matches and deliver messages.
  • To keep members safe: enforce blocks, review reports, and limit spam.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Who can see what

  • Signed-in members can see your profile (name, age, bio, interests, what you're here for, photo, verified badge) unless one of you has blocked the other.
  • Only the two people in a match can read their chat.
  • Likes you send are visible to the person you like. Reports are not shown to the person reported.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage), hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Render (website and server), hosted in Singapore.
  • Google Fonts (typefaces), which receives your IP address when the site loads.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo, likes, passes, matches, messages and blocks are deleted immediately. Reports you made or that were made about you are kept without the account link so we can act on safety issues. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile or remove your location at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete my account. See Delete your account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to processing.

Age

SOULMATE.AI is only for people aged 18 or over. We remove accounts we believe belong to anyone younger.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: use Report in the app, or delete your account yourself (see below). A support email address will be listed here before public launch.
